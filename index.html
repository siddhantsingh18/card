A Letter From My Soul

Words from my heart

May 2025 — Written just for you

My darling, my love, my mother's day queen,

There are women who are called mothers by the children they bring into this world. And then there is you — a woman so full of love that you mother everyone around you, especially me.

You have held me when I was broken, celebrated me when I was winning, and quietly loved me through every ordinary Tuesday in between. You take care of me not because you have to, but because your heart simply doesn't know any other way to be.

On this Mother's Day, I want you to know: the world is warmer because you are in it. My world is possible because of you. You are a mother in the truest, most beautiful sense — not just to a child, but to the love that lives between us.

Thank you for every meal you made, every time you checked if I was okay, every hug that fixed what words couldn't. Thank you for being my safe place in a world that can be so loud.

With every heartbeat — yours, always. 🌹
